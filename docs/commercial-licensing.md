# Commercial Licensing

FormaBot uses a two-track licensing model:

1. The repository's **FormaBot Personal Use License 1.0** permits individual, personal, noncommercial use.
2. Commercial or organizational use requires a separately negotiated written agreement. Contact the [repository owner](https://github.com/rouicezar) with the intended use, number of users, deployment model, and distribution requirements. Do not post confidential customer information publicly.

A request is not authorization. Pricing, support, redistribution, hosted use, and the scope of rights must be established in the signed agreement. User outputs are not automatically owned by the project.

The personal-use license is deliberately more restrictive than standard noncommercial licenses that also permit some organizational use. It is not an OSI-approved open-source license. The license cannot revoke independently granted rights in third-party dependencies; required notices must remain in distributions. Confirm contributor ownership and obtain professional legal review before executing commercial agreements.
